Exit mechanism — money path

Every other control on this page changes what this account is entitled to. This one changes how its live positions exit: on anything but default, once the mechanism comes onside the engine cancels the evaluator's SL and TP ladder and re-parks a stop at the mechanism's level on every closed bar.

Two switches are required. This one, and the engine-wide Live trail governor on Control → Engine. Set here with the master switch off and nothing happens — the flash will say so rather than reporting a bare success. Watch it run on Signals → Trail governor.

The mechanism's stop is frequently wider than the stop the trade was sized for (54% of measured SAR handovers), so a position can carry more risk than its designed SL implies. The measured edge over the current exit did not exclude zero.

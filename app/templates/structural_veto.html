Phase 4 of the price-action program, and the application with leverage: structurally-triggered paths are 0.62% of the enqueued book and none of them delivered, so a standalone structural signal takes weeks to reach a readable sample. A veto needs no new signal at all — it is measurable against ~97% of the book from the day it ships, and its answer is the precondition for the standalone lane being worth building.

"Opposing" is signed toward the trade throughout: resistance above entry for a long, support below for a short. A directional feature that is not signed toward the trade scores half the book backwards, and the book is ~50/50 by side — so the error would not show as an empty column, it would just make the feature look like noise.

Structural snap{% if filter_setup %} — {{ filter_setup }}{% endif %}

Every live evaluator computes its stop and its targets arithmetically — a buffer off a moving average or a prior bar, then fixed R-multiples off that distance. Nothing in that chain asks where price has actually traded, so TP1 can land a hair beyond a swing high that has rejected four times. This lane stamps, on every enqueued signal, where the nearest swing level or round number sat relative to the geometry that actually shipped.

The repair has existed in the engine's structural_levels.py since it was written, and build_channel_signal called it — behind a guard on candle_highs is not None that no caller ever satisfied, under a comment claiming every evaluator passed the arrays. It never ran. It now runs at the one point the geometry is final, and applies nothing until a path is named in the ops allow-list.

Where the levels came from

Two generators with different standing. A swing is a price the market actually traded and rejected. A round number is a psychological level we inject ourselves, on an absolute grid — 0.2% wide at $50,000 and 20% wide at $0.05, where it can never fall inside a stop band and contributes nothing. An all-swing column at sub-cent prices is the grid being inert, not round numbers being unhelpful.

Scoring timeframe — what each path is actually being read on

A second defect found in the same audit, and a wider one. Scanner._get_primary_timeframe was return "5m" for every channel since it was written — a constant wearing a lookup's docstring — and six money-path consumers read it: continuation-sweep evidence (which feeds the 25-point SMC dimension), the VWAP extension gate, the OI + funding gate, cross-timeframe volume divergence, the chart-pattern confidence bonus (the 10-point Patterns dimension), and the volume inputs to the composite score.

So for every setup that does not trade 5m, all six readings were taken on a different chart than the trade. Nothing crashed and nothing was empty.

Why there is no combined number

The two arms are not measured on the same population and are not equally knowable. Averaging a fully-decidable TP1 delta with a partly-decidable SL delta would produce one figure whose value moves with the SL arm's refusal rate rather than with the mechanism — and the refusals are loss-selected on one side and win-selected on the other, so the blend would be biased in a direction that changes with the market. A route test asserts this page never grows a blended key.

Also load-bearing: max_favorable_excursion_pct and max_adverse_excursion_pct are updated on the monitor's mark-price ticks, not from intrabar candle extremes. A touch between ticks is not recorded, so every "the snapped level was reached" verdict is conservative — this lane can under-count rescues and can never invent one. That bias points against the snap, which is the safe direction for an adoption decision, but it is a bias.

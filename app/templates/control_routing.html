Path routing — live ⇄ dark

One row per path and side, and both directions in one place. Live → dark diverts a (path, side) to the dark feed: subscribers stop receiving it and it keeps being measured. Dark → live is a per-path promotion rule that lets a slice of the dark feed through; rules are edited on Promotions. A diverted path is never deleted — it keeps producing evidence, so the decision can be re-read on fresh data.

How the two directions interact

  • Divert to dark takes effect on the next scan and needs no confirm: it stops delivery and places no order. It is written to the engine's retired_paths list — the same value Control → Tunables edits — and this page reads the engine back after every change and reports what the engine says, not what was clicked.
  • Restore to live needs the confirm: it puts the path back in front of paid subscribers, and auto-trade users take its orders.
  • A diverted row enters the dark feed with the gate retired:<PATH>:<SIDE>. A dark → live rule whose gates are Any matches that gate and puts the row straight back on the live feed — that is what a CONFLICT marks. To send a path live again, restore it here rather than promoting around its own retirement.

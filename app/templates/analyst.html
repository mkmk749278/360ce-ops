{% call info("What is compared, and what is not") %}

The analyst rows are its dark walk: realized per setup posted, an unfilled limit counted at 0.00%.

Does the model add anything?

{% call info("How to read this") %}

The hourly analyst picks levels by id from the engine's menu. Every accepted setup is walked forward dark on 5m bars, under several arms over the same opportunity. Nothing here reached a subscriber or an order.

The refutation condition: if the fixed rule (same symbol and side, levels picked mechanically from the same menu) matches the analyst net of fees, the model added nothing and we ship the rule.

Realized is what a posted order earned: a limit the market never reached earned 0.00%, and a partial tranche earns its return times the share that filled. Net of a flat 0.07% round trip. Cost model charges maker 0.02% / taker 0.05% by order type, plus funding from the derivatives recorder at the symbol's own settlement times. Slippage is not modelled. A row whose funding the recorder cannot price is left out, so the cost model is shown beside the flat-fee result over the same rows: their gap is cost alone. Read how many of those rows were fills.

Before walk v2 (2026-11-27) the headline scored an unfilled limit at the market outcome. That population is selected: a limit goes unfilled because price ran away from it, so its market outcome is conditionally good. It is shown on its own below and is never an expected result.

Stop first on an ambiguous bar; no target on a limit's fill bar. Read n before any average, the without-top-1% column beside it, and days +: alts move with BTC, so a day of same-side setups is close to one bet.

The analyst arm, taken apart

{% call info("Why this card exists") %}

Limits that filled beside limits that never filled, scored at the market outcome. The second figure is a counterfactual over a selected population; the realized headline counts those rows at 0.00%.

A filled setup has three endings, not two: the first target, the stop, or the 12h time exit. An expectancy written as p(target) × R − (1 − p) books every time exit as a full loss.

No valid trade: at submit the market was already past the setup's own first target (or stop), so a market entry has no geometry. It scores 0.00% and is counted. Before 2026-11-25 these rows were walked and booked a "target" at a loss; the engine has corrected them.

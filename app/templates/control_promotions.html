Dark → live promotion

Each path below has been emitting into the dark feed — real signals the scanner was willing to send, carried past one gate and diverted before the queue. This page is where a named slice of that population stops being diverted. A promotion removes only the gate the row was carried past: every other gate above it already said yes, and the router's second layer (correlation lock, cooldowns, concurrency caps, same-direction throttle) still runs below.

Measuring does not stop. A promoted row is still written to the dark ledger and still walked, marked promoted with its delivery outcome stamped — so the evidence keeps arriving after the decision instead of freezing at the moment it was taken. The two populations are never pooled anywhere: a promoted row reached the router and a dark row did not.

Retention scores a pair on opportunity (is it still producing candidates) and liveness (is the move still trading above its own baseline) — and deliberately never on outcomes. A dark row resolves up to six hours later, so profitability is not knowable inside a promotion window; and a pair dropped for bad outcomes stops producing candidates, so it can never earn its way back. Verdicts: warmup = too young to judge (under {{ '{:.0f}'.format((rb.get('min_hold_sec') or 0) / 60.0) }} min) · hold = inside its window, behaving · extend = still producing past the flat TTL, keep it · release = give the slot back, for the named reason. Release reasons: no_candidates (scanned ≥{{ rb.get('min_scans_to_judge') }} times, produced nothing) · move_spent (back at its own baseline for ≥{{ '{:.0f}'.format((rb.get('spent_for_sec') or 0) / 60.0) }} min) · ttl (hit the {{ '{:.0f}'.format((rb.get('max_hold_sec') or 0) / 3600.0) }}h ceiling — a bound a good score cannot talk its way past). A pair whose activity cannot be measured is held, never dropped: absence of a reading is not evidence a move is over.

Unlock shorts DARK

{% call info("What this lane measures", wide=true) %}

Short the two weeks before a large team / investor token unlock: enter at the close of T−14, cover at the close of T+2. An unlock qualifies at ≥ {{ '%.1f'|format((report.rule.get('min_fraction') or 0.005) * 100) }}% of max supply, to insiders, on a Binance USDT perp.

Why: insiders hold tokens with a near-zero cost basis and the date is public, so the market leans on the price beforehand. Research: {{ report.rule.get('research_doc') or 'docs/SHORTS_MARKET_RESEARCH_2026_09_25.md' }}.

Dark means dark. Nothing here reaches a subscriber, the signal queue or an order. The engine reads public market data and the DefiLlama vesting calendar and writes one ledger; this page renders it.

Two squeeze filters are stamped, never applied — every unlock is walked, so the rule and the filtered rule are measured side by side: crowded (last settled funding ≤ {{ '%.2f'|format((report.rule.get('crowded_funding') or -0.0003) * 100) }}%) and running (up more than {{ '%.0f'|format((report.rule.get('running_ret_14d') or 0.2) * 100) }}% in the 14 days into entry). They were exploratory in the research; this lane is how they earn or lose their place.

Every figure is the short's PnL in % of notional: positive means the short made money. Fees ({{ report.rule.get('fee_rt_pct', 0.07) }}%) and slippage ({{ report.rule.get('slip_rt_pct', 0.10) }}%) per round trip and the funding actually settled while the row was open are all charged.

{% endcall %}

The one short mechanism that survived twelve months of research, measured forward on real prices before anyone may trade it.

{% call info("How to read the open rows") %}

Freshness leads. A live mark is only honest beside a row that can say whether it is still being walked: behind is how many hourly bars the engine's walk is short of now, graded on the engine's own stamps. Three or more is STALLED.

The mark is this page's own live price (one request for the whole book). The unrealized figure is gross — fees are charged at close. Worst so far is how far price has risen above entry: the squeeze the stops are measured against.

A stop shows hit once the engine's walk has seen a bar touch it. A negative room with no hit means the mark has crossed the level and the walk has not reached that bar yet.

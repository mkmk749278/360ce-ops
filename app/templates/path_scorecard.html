Per-path verdict — is this path earning its place, and can we tell yet?

What this is

Every (setup_class, side) of the delivered book — signals subscribers actually received — with n, distinct symbols, net %, a symbol-clustered 95% interval and a verdict. It is the standing version of the analysis the 2026-08-13 retirements were decided from, which was run by hand, once.

Why it exists now. On 2026-08-30 the same-direction and per-channel caps came off so every path could reach the feed rather than MOVER_TREND_PULLBACK holding the slots by arithmetic. It worked — MVRTP's share fell 76% → 59% and every other path is running 3–7× its old delivery rate. Over the ten days that followed the delivered book went from +0.506%/trade (310 trades, 21 days) to −0.113%/trade (374 trades, 10 days), and nothing in either repo could say which path, if any, was responsible.

The sample floor is applied before the interval, engine side. A cell under the floor reads INSUFFICIENT however decisive its interval looks — the worst-looking cell in the current window is three trades with an interval excluding zero, which is FAILED_AUCTION_RECLAIM's +0.846R on three rows with the sign flipped. The interval is still shown, because hiding it would leave you wondering; it just cannot produce a finding.

Ops computes none of this. The interval, the floor and the verdict are the engine's — a second implementation of a statistical rule is a mirror that drifts in the flattering direction, and this one would drift toward retiring paths.

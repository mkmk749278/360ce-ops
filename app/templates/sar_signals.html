RUNNING — stamped, still inside its 48h measurement window (no exit yet, so R and PnL are blank rather than zero). CLOSED_TRAIL — the moving stop caught price. CLOSED_WINDOW — 48h passed without the trail firing; marked to the window's close. vs live — this trade's R minus what the live geometry (its paired @SARBASE arm) made on the same entry; positive means the trail would have done better on this signal. Blank until both arms resolve. Source — three stages, and the middle one used to be miscounted as the first (fixed 2026-07-25, when this page reported 98 “emitted” for a window with 3 real signals). DELIVERED means the router confirmed delivery and a subscriber saw it. QUEUED means it passed every scanner gate and the queue took it, but the router's own caps then dropped it — nobody saw it. SUPPRESSED means a scanner gate killed it. All three are measured; only DELIVERED can justify changing what users receive. Current / Unreal % — live mark from Binance for trades still running, so an open trade isn't a blank row for two days. It is not the SAR arm's result: both arms share this entry, so the unrealized move is identical for both and says nothing about which exit wins. That is why it sits in its own columns and never in R / PnL % / vs live, which stay blank until the trail actually exits. Blank here means the price was unavailable, not that the trade is flat.

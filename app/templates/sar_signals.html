SAR agreement at entry

A trailing SAR is a stop that is already somewhere when the trade opens, so agreement at entry decides which exit governs. Pointing our way: the level sits behind the entry and the trail rides from bar one — the exit method being measured. Pointing the other way: the trade runs on its live SL and TP1 instead, exactly like the control arm, and hands over to the trail only if the indicator later comes onside. If it never does, the live geometry closes the trade and this arm and the control agree exactly. So the two buckets answer different questions and the pooled headline would move with the agreement mix rather than with the exit — which is why they are split here. Handovers is the count that matters most: a row that never handed over contributes nothing to the comparison, so the A/B is decided by that population alone, however large the totals look.

Clear the ledger

Drops every stamped and resolved row and starts the measurement window over. Irreversible — there is no undo and no export taken on your behalf, so pull the CSV first if you want the window kept. Use this when the rows are evidence of a defect rather than of the exit method: a walker replaying the wrong candles, a scoring change mid-window, anything that makes the population untrustworthy. Leaving bad rows in place poisons the edge-matrix cells they pool into, which is why clearing beats ignoring. The engine owns the ledger and does the clearing; in isolated mode it applies within ~15s. Recorded in the audit log.

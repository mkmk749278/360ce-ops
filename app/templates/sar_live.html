This is not a replay. Every arm here was advanced inside the engine's monitor loop while the trade was open, so an open row carries the stop the mechanism would have parked right now — as long as the row says it is still advancing. An arm whose symbol has stopped receiving klines keeps the stop it last computed, and the price beside it is still live, so a stalled row would otherwise read as a fresh one. Check Last advance before Dist. to stop. That is the one thing neither SAR signals (a 48h replay) nor Dark signals (a kline replay after the engine already closed the trade) can show, and it is what a rollout decision actually needs.

The mechanism. At generation, if SAR already agrees with the signal it governs from bar one and the original SL and TP1 are never used — the trade exits when SAR flips. If SAR opposes, the original SL and TP1 stay live until SAR comes onside; at that moment they are cancelled and SAR takes over. If SAR never comes onside, the trade closes on its original stop, or in full at TP1. Measured on 5m and 15m as independent arms on the same signals, so the timeframe question and the mechanism question share one window.

These arms place no orders and change no exit. Live exits still run on the signal's SL/TP and the FSM. Nothing on this page has touched anyone's capital.

Two fills, and why neither is called “the” result

“Close at market when SAR flips” leaves no resting stop between bars, which would breach the never-a-naked-position rule the moment it went live — so the arm models a stop parked at the SAR level and amended each bar, and records both prices: Fill @level is that stop being touched intrabar (a gap through it fills at the bar's open — worse, never better); Fill @confirm is waiting for the bar to close and exiting at market. Confirm cost is the difference. That number has never been measured anywhere in this system, and picking one fill before it is known would be choosing the answer rather than measuring it.

R is reported against two denominators, and neither is called “the” R. R divides by the SL distance at entry, which keeps it comparable with the edge matrix and Track record — but when SAR governs, that stop is cancelled, and SAR's own has been wider than it on more than half of all handovers. R @risk divides by the risk the arm actually parked. A loss taken at 2.7× the designed risk reads −1.90R against the design and −0.71R against the exposure; both are true and quoting one silently picks the answer. Figures are gross — no fees, no funding — so a row reading flat is a real cost live.

Did these arms step forward, or walk history?

An arm anchors to the newest closed bar the store holds when it opens. If that bar is itself hours old — a promoted mover on REST re-seed with no WS klines — the arm reads SAR-at-entry off a stale bar and its first advance walks the whole gap in one pass, stamping a fresh Last advance on every bar of it. ACHUSDT 15m did exactly that on 2026-07-31: 158 bars consumed in ten bars of life, published as a live fill under the sentence “this is not a replay”. The engine now refuses to open an arm on a stale anchor; this panel grades what is already in the ledger.

…and how much of the delivered book did it get to look at?

Every panel above grades arms that exist. None of them can see a delivered signal that never became an arm at all — and that was the largest exclusion of the lot, with no counter anywhere in either repo until now. A guest-session audit on 2026-08-08 joined this ledger to the closed-signal record over the arm window and found 124 of 152 delivered trades armed (81.6%), while the 28 without ran −1.643%/trade at 10.7% win (67.9% SL_HIT) against +0.753% and 43.5% for the armed ones. The verdict below was therefore measured on a winner-enriched subset and read as the mechanism's result on our book. That is this page's own rule — what fraction of the population resolved, and is the unresolved part random? — applied one step too late.

Was the SAR stop inside the risk the trade was sized for?

When SAR agrees at entry it governs and the signal's own SL is never used, so an arm can end up carrying more risk than the evaluator sized the trade for. The first two live arms did exactly that on one signal — MUUUSDT SHORT, designed SL 3.00%, 5m SAR at 1.60% and 15m SAR at 3.77%. R divides by the SL distance at entry, so a wider-stop arm mechanically produces bigger losses per stop-out: pooled, "the exit timed badly" and "the exit risked more" would arrive as one number wearing the other's name. Split here so they don't.

Max profit before the original stop — and why it is not the MFE column

Every other excursion figure on this page is bounded by the SAR arm's own exit. The loop stops advancing mfe_pct the moment SAR flips out, so on a row that flipped early it says how far the trade ran before SAR closed it and is structurally silent on everything after. Rendering that under the words "max profit" would be a number that is always about right and never means what it says — worse than a blank, because a blank prompts a question. So the engine walks a second arm over the same bars with the SAR exit removed: it holds to the trade's original stop and records the peak on the way. The two are different measurements over different windows and are never blended.

Dist. to stop is how far price sits from the parked SAR level — the only column on any SAR surface that says “this mechanism is about to act”. It is blank while the original SL/TP is still governing, because distance-to-SAR means nothing before the handover. Realized columns stay empty on this tab by construction: an unrealized number in a realized column is how a still-open trade gets read as a finished one.

Last advance is when the arm last consumed a closed bar — not when the file was written, and not when the price was fetched. Those are three different clocks and only this one says the stop beside it is current. A stalled row's Dist. to stop compares a live price against a level computed bars ago, and crossed means price is already through that level with the arm still open — a mechanism that had actually been stepped would have exited. The engine retires a stalled arm as INSUFFICIENT / candle_feed_stalled once the gap in its bars is unrecoverable, so it is excluded from the verdict rather than scored across bars nobody saw.

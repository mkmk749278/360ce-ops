Why there is no R here. This page led with R until 2026-08-03, and R described 6% of it: 421 of 448 trades in the owner's 30d window carried no entry-risk stamp, so the R total, the R average and the win rate above them were all computed on 27 rows while the average move beside them covered all 448, and the page never said the two described different populations. R also equalises nothing here — the engine sizes every position at a fixed notional, so the stop distance appears nowhere in the sizing and a 0.80% loss and a 6.14% loss both read as exactly one R of loss while costing $4.00 and $30.70 of the same $500. A percentage needs no denominator, so it cannot shrink its own population that way. The Strategy Lab and the edge matrix remain the R-keyed surfaces.

R, not portfolio return. R is the realised move divided by the signal's own stop distance — the same denominator the SAR arm and the edge matrix use, so a number here is comparable with one there. A portfolio percentage is deliberately absent: it needs an assumed position size, and the router's 3-same-direction cap means two users on identical settings get different fills, so it would not be a fact about anyone.

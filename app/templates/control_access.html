Read-only access

Codes that open the measurement and diagnostic pages only — the feed, every lane, every export, the truth report. A code cannot reach this control panel, the diag runner, the subscriber tables, the raw data volume or the /api/v1 token surface, and it cannot issue any write: a read-only session is refused on every method except GET, and on every route not explicitly classified as readable (app/guest_scope.py).

A read-only holder still sees real trading data — every signal, position and PnL figure on the pages above. This tier withholds the control plane and subscriber data, not the book.

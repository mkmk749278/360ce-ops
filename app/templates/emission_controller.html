Layer G — autonomous emission controller

The outer loop of the Autonomous Portfolio: it reads the measured gate verdicts and the Strategy×Context edge matrix and moves the per-strategy emission overrides itself, with no human in the loop, inside a bounded envelope. It has been live on the money path since S72 — this page is the first surface that shows what it is doing.

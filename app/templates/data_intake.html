Data intake — what we are actually reading from Binance

Built so this question never needs an SSH session again. Every figure is computed engine-side from state the engine already holds — no new vendor calls, no polling, and no clock of ours: a surface may not grade its own liveness on a clock it supplies.

Provenance leads freshness. Age answers "is this current". Source answers "is this the thing I think it is" — and every finding that produced this page was the second kind: a value of the right shape, arriving from somewhere other than where its consumers assume.

Footprint — volume at price, per bar

Phase 2b. OrderFlowStore gives one signed number per bar. It says whether takers were net buying, and is structurally silent on where in the bar they were buying, whether a level absorbed them, and how the aggression was sized. A bar that grinds up on steady small buying and one that spikes into a wall and fails produce the same delta. This keeps buy and sell volume at each price, which is what turns that number into a shape.

Magnitudes, never verdicts. Nothing here emits "absorption = true". Every value is a ratio or a component, because a boolean bakes in a threshold and a threshold chosen now would be chosen from no data at all — Phases 4–5 pick thresholds from the distribution this phase exists to create.

Entry features — MVRTP

MOVER_TREND_PULLBACK takes its entry from price against three simple moving averages (SMA7 / SMA25 / SMA99 on 15m) plus one ATR for the stop buffer. It reads the 15m volume series and passes it only to consol_break — the two triggers that carry almost all the volume, fast_pullback and deep_pullback, never look at volume at all. Everything below was already sitting in smc_data at that same call and was never read.

Nothing on this page is applied. Every “keep” column is a question, not a change. The engine emits the identical signal whether this measurement is on or off, and a test on the producing side pins that by comparing the emitted signal both ways.

Read the n and the kept-fraction before the Δ. A rule that keeps 95% of the book has not been tested by this window whatever its Δ says, and a rule whose Δ rests on three trades is not a finding — the window that prompted this work had 46 MVRTP signals, 19 tested cells, and exactly one cleared 95% in the backwards direction against a ~62% chance of a spurious hit. Unknown rows are counted apart on purpose: a rule cannot be credited with an outcome it could not have seen, and folding them into “keep” is how a filter takes credit for rows it never filtered.

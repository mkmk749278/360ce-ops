Router drops — the last hop before a subscriber

Every other funnel in this dashboard stops at enqueue. The scanner's own path funnel increments its emitted stage right after _enqueue_signal succeeds, so that column counts enqueues and has been labelled emitted the whole time. SignalRouter._process then rejects on twelve further conditions before anything reaches a channel, a push, the app feed or an order. This page is that hop.

A drop is not a fault. Every gate here is doing its job — the concurrency caps are blast-radius protection and nothing on this page is an argument to widen them. It answers two separate questions: where the volume goes, and whether one path is absorbing a shared budget that other paths need.

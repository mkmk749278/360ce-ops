Live runtime knobs. A change applies within 5 seconds, survives restarts, and needs no redeploy.

How this works

These write to the engine's control/runtime_tunables store — the engine picks changes up within 5 seconds, survives restarts, and never needs a .env edit. The 2026-07-07 exit overhaul ships ACTIVE with every knob controlled from here; its defaults were calibrated on the 7-day study (52% of stops were shakeouts; 84% of break-even scratches were winners that resumed).

Categories are collapsed; open the one you need, or filter. Filtering hides rows, it does not exclude them from a save — a hidden field still posts its current value, so applying a category always writes that whole category exactly as it stands.

An edit you have not applied is marked in blue and counted on the Apply bar, which follows you down an open category. Click a description to read all of it.

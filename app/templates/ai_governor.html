AI Trade Governor — what it decided, and what it refused

What this lane is

An asynchronous critic attached to already-open positions. The deterministic FSM places the trade; this asks a model, on the signal's own bar clock, whether reality still supports the premise — and it may answer only MAINTAIN, ADJUST_TP, ADJUST_SL or PANIC_CLOSE. It never emits a price: the engine builds a menu of pre-validated levels and the model returns a key, so a hallucinated choice is bounded by a menu we wrote.

Keyed on signals, not positions — one verdict fans out to every user in the trade, so the model cost is flat in members. The apply path is not: one PANIC_CLOSE at 1,000 members is ~1,000 Firestore reads and ~5,000 signed Binance calls in a burst, from the IP that got rate-limited on 2026-09-01. That is why it carries a second, separate budget.

MAINTAIN is recorded like every other outcome. A lane that logs only its interventions cannot compute a baseline and will look brilliant.

There is no combined "did the governor help" figure here, and there will not be one. Three of the four arms are decidable from the closed-signal record and one is not: a wider stop on a loser asks whether price would have come back, and the walk ended at the stop; a tighter stop on a winner asks whether the drawdown preceded the target, and MFE and MAE carry no ordering between them. Those remove opposite ends of the distribution, so one number over all four would move with the SL arm's refusal rate rather than with the mechanism.

The bound is derived from the loop, not configured: the floor above, the slowest recent tick x1.5, and a hard 60s cap. A verdict is queued in one tick and drained in the next, so a fixed bound smaller than the tick refuses everything — which is what 7-of-8 aged out meant, on a loop measured at 7–20s against a 10s constant. Both numbers render because a reader needs to know which one binds. Measured for every verdict, MAINTAIN included. MAINTAIN returns before the staleness check, so the refusal is only ever observable on the arms that would have acted — measured only there, an "all of them aged out" reading is a fact about those arms rather than about the lane's clock, and there is no denominator to read it against.

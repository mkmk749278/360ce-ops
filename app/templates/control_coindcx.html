CoinDCX

{% call info("What this page is") %}

A second exchange for auto-trade, for Indian users, fed by the same signals as Binance. A user trades on ONE exchange, the one they chose in the app. Every CoinDCX trade uses one exit: the whole position closes at TP1 or at the stop, and both are placed on CoinDCX the moment the entry fills.

It ships dark: COINDCX_EXECUTION_ENABLED is off, so the engine places no CoinDCX order for anyone. Opening it is an engine .env change and a deploy, deliberately not a button here. The self-test below is the step before that.

Every number on this page is the engine's (coindcx_status.json, rewritten every reconcile cycle). Ops only grades it.

Who can trade on CoinDCX

{% call info("How access works") %}

Two switches and a list, all held by the engine and changed here — nothing is edited on the server.

Master switch: while it is off, no CoinDCX order is placed for anyone. Positions already open keep their stop on CoinDCX either way.

Allow-list: while "open to all" is off, only these users are traded. An empty list means nobody — emptying it can never open CoinDCX by accident.

Open to all: every user who chose CoinDCX in the app and connected a key. The public-launch step.

Add users by the phone number they signed in with. The engine finds their account; you never handle an ID.

Real-account self-test

{% call info("What the test does, step by step") %}

Run on YOUR CoinDCX account, before any user. It reads the wallet, checks the pair is tradable and flat, sets 2x leverage, opens the exchange's minimum position at market, places the stop and the target together, reads both back off the position, exits, and confirms no order is left behind.

It proves what CoinDCX's documentation cannot: that a stop and a target rest together on one position, and that nothing survives the exit. The engine refuses to run it unless the uid is in COINDCX_EXECUTION_ALLOWED_UIDS, and refuses a pair you already hold.

Cost: two taker fees on the minimum notional, plus any move during the few seconds the position is open.

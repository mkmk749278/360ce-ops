Signal Profit Tracker

A parallel "what if we'd held to the stop" view — real trading is unchanged. Every signal is replayed on Binance 1m candles from dispatch, exiting only at its original stop. Max profit is the best it ever showed (peak-to-entry); Result is the held-to-stop outcome (the SL loss once stopped, else live P/L). Real exit is what the engine actually did — the gap between the two is the give-back.

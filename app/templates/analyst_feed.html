How a card is scored

{% call info("Scoring rules") %}

State and PnL are the engine's analyst arm: a limit order at the first entry price, exiting at the first target or the stop, else a time exit after 12h. Later targets are the analyst's own and are shown, not booked.

Net of a 0.07% round trip. Stop counts first on a 5m bar that touched both levels, and a target is never booked on the bar that filled the limit.

Not filled means the market never came back to the limit before expiry. Its PnL is what entering at market would have made, and it is counted apart from real fills.

Live mark, unrealised PnL and distances use Binance's last price and are never part of a realised figure. The walk advances on 5m bar closes, so a level the mark has just crossed books on the next close.
